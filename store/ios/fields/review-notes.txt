Madeira Ativa is a guide to hiking trails, sport events and local history on Madeira, Portugal. There is no account, no login, no in-app purchase, no advertising and no user-generated content. Nothing is gated, so no demo credentials are needed - every screen is reachable from a cold launch.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.9.3
- German interface, alongside English and Portuguese (EN / PT / DE switch in the header).
- Island buses: the orange "Island bus timetables" banner on Home opens which trails are reachable by bus, the stops at each end and the next departures.
- A lighter Home screen: "The island now" (weather, sea, tides, airport wind, ships in port), trail status and webcams.

GUIDELINE 4.2 - WHAT IS NATIVE
Home, Events, Map, Levadas and News are native tabs, as are trail detail (elevation profile, official time and difficulty, railings and tunnels, car parks and buses at each end), race statistics, the history section (Roots) and TV broadcasts. The Map tab is a native map view with OpenStreetMap raster tiles, switchable event and trail layers and trail geometry drawn as polylines. Native OS integrations: EventKit for "Add to calendar", local notifications (a reminder the evening before an event you follow, and an alert when a trail opens or closes), the system share sheet, haptics, on-device favourites, and an offline cache so the app still works in the mountains.

Some screens embed web content we publish at azenha.ai: the Ask AI tab, the bus timetables, the webcam list, and the optional WebGL terrain views ("Heat", "3D", "3D flyover", "Aerial").

ASK AI (BETA)
Ask AI answers questions about trails, the weather and events. The app first assembles facts from its own live data (trail status, official times, forecasts, IPMA warnings, events), shows them on screen, and a language model writes a short summary from those facts only. Every answer is labelled "AI" with the note "AI answers can be wrong - the facts below are the source", and the facts stay visible underneath. Questions are not tied to any user identity. Try the chip "Where to go today?".

INDEPENDENCE AND SOURCES
Madeira Ativa is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Regional Government of Madeira or the Madeira tourism board. Trail names, PR codes and open/closed status come from the public notice-to-walkers list published by visitmadeira.com. Weather from Open-Meteo, warnings from IPMA. Map data is (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). News items credit the publisher and link to the original article.

TESTING NOTE
The app needs a connection on first launch to populate Events, News and Levadas from our public feed, then serves the cached copy offline. If a feed is unreachable the tab shows a retry state.
