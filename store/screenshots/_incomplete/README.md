# Portuguese screenshot sets — incomplete, do not upload

ios-ipad13-pt holds four or five frames of a six-frame set. It is parked here
rather than in screenshots/ so that everything under screenshots/ is safe to
drag into App Store Connect without checking.

Nothing is lost by leaving it: a localisation with no screenshots of its own
falls back to the primary language's, so the Portuguese listing shows the
English screenshots until a full Portuguese set exists. The listing text,
keywords, subtitle and promotional text are already Portuguese — those are what
the store search actually reads.

To finish it: run the app on a simulator, tap PT in the app bar, and shoot the
same six screens as ios-6.5 / ios-ipad13.
